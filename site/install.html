Install Kevin

Why bother?

He opens in one tap like an app, talks back out loud, and keeps your shared shelf (the things you and your friends riffed) on your phone. It takes ten seconds and no app store.

How

  • iPhone or iPad (Safari): tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Android (Chrome): tap Install, or the menu, then Install app.
  • Mac (Safari): File, then Add to Dock.
  • Windows or Mac (Chrome or Edge): click the install icon at the right end of the address bar.
  • Firefox: it can't install apps; open roastedbykevin.com in Chrome, Edge or Safari.

Desktop Kevin

Kevin, small, floating on your laptop screen. He riffs when you click him and now and then judges your font. It shows the live Kevin, so nothing of Kevin runs on your computer, and it asks for no permissions.

  • Mac (Kevin-Desktop-mac.dmg): open it, drag Kevin to Applications, then open Kevin. macOS says it can't check him: click Done, open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, scroll down to "Kevin was blocked" and click Open Anyway, then Open. (On macOS 14 or older: right-click Kevin, choose Open, then Open.)
  • Windows (Kevin-Desktop-win.exe): no install, just double-click. If SmartScreen says it protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway.
  • Turn on "Let Kevin see what's playing" and he riffs along with your music and shows: only the title, the app and play or pause, never other windows, never stored. Quiet for an hour and Not during this are in his menu.
  • Kevin stays out of the way until something plays or you call him (Cmd+Shift+K on a Mac, Ctrl+Shift+K on Windows). He talks out loud; Mute is on him and in his menu. He listens only while you hold the talk button, keeps no recordings, and goes gentle for kids' shows.
  • Got a tester link or a remix? Copy it, then pick "Use my Kevin link" from Kevin's menu (menu bar on a Mac, tray on Windows).

Unsigned while Kevin is new; the source is public. No laptop app? Kevin small, in a tab works too.

Why it's safe

Nothing you say is saved. Kevin never asks for money, logins or your location. The installed app is the same page in your browser's app sandbox; it installs nothing else. The real Kevin lives only at roastedbykevin.com: if an install link points anywhere else, it isn't Kevin. Check the stamp.
