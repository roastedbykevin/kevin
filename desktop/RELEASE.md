Kevin, small, on your screen. He floats above your windows, riffs when you click him, and now and then judges your font. Same Kevin as https://roastedbykevin.com: the app shows the live site, so nothing of Kevin runs on your computer, and it asks for no permissions (no mic, camera or location).

**Mac (Kevin-Desktop-mac.dmg):** open the .dmg and drag Kevin to Applications. The first time, right-click Kevin in Applications and choose Open, then Open again. Kevin lives in the menu bar (the little face): Show, Hide, Quiet for an hour, Quit.

**Windows (Kevin-Desktop-win.exe):** no install; double-click it. The first time, Windows SmartScreen may say it protected your PC: click More info, then Run anyway. Kevin lives in the tray by the clock.

**Your own Kevin link** (a tester link or a remix): copy it, then choose "Use my Kevin link" from Kevin's menu.
