Kevin, small, on your screen: the theater row, made real. He floats above your windows, riffs when you click him, and now and then judges your font.

**New in 0.2: Kevin riffs along with what's playing.** Turn on "Let Kevin see what's playing" (he asks once; it's also in his menu). He then reads only the title, the app and play or pause from Spotify, Apple Music, Apple TV, VLC, and browser tabs on YouTube, Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+, Max, Hulu and Apple TV. Never other windows, never screenshots, and titles are never stored. While something plays he heckles its craft every few minutes, cheers or groans when it changes, and offers Riff along and Make a card. Ignore him three times and he riffs half as often; "Quiet for an hour" and "Not during this" are one click away, and he stays out of full-screen video unless you tap him in. On a Mac, the first time he reads an app, macOS asks whether Kevin may control it: say OK to the apps you want him to watch along with. Same Kevin as https://roastedbykevin.com: the app shows the live site, so nothing of Kevin runs on your computer, and it asks for no browser permissions (no mic, camera or location).

**Mac (Kevin-Desktop-mac.dmg):** open the .dmg and drag Kevin to Applications. The first time, right-click Kevin in Applications and choose Open, then Open again. Kevin lives in the menu bar (the little face): Show, Hide, Quiet for an hour, Quit.

**Windows (Kevin-Desktop-win.exe):** no install; double-click it. The first time, Windows SmartScreen may say it protected your PC: click More info, then Run anyway. Kevin lives in the tray by the clock.

**Your own Kevin link** (a tester link or a remix): copy it, then choose "Use my Kevin link" from Kevin's menu.
